Strategic Themes This Week

Cross-Portfolio Synthesis
01

Memorial Day Capital Reserve

Multiple accounts are deliberately under-pacing to maximize the May 25 demand window. Spend curves are timed to compress against peak intent rather than smooth-deliver across the month.

  • LumeCube — scaling toward $2K/day
  • POPFLEX — finalizing creative assets
  • Chilly Goat — promo already lifting MER to 5.06
  • House of Leon — pacing 77.5%, holding for promo
02

Branded Defense Posture

Competitor conquesting on brand-term auctions is widespread. Branded CPCs are inflating 10–14% across multiple accounts, requiring bid-cap discipline to defend market share without eroding margin.

  • B2M — +14% branded CPC
  • House of Leon — +12% branded CPC
  • Tie Boss — +10% core search CPC
  • Iron Neck — rising Alpha Harness CPA
03

PMAX & Audience Refinement

Performance Max is the recurring optimization theme — exclusions, asset refreshes, and high-LTV signaling are unlocking next-leg efficiency where the algorithm has drifted broad.

  • POPFLEX — excluded 6.6K junk YouTube links
  • Meditac — lifestyle imagery refresh queued
  • Luxe Lens — focus on top 10% margin SKUs
  • Omegavia — high-LTV audience signaling
